How are aptitude buckets decided?

We follow uma-rpg Classes v3: compare all four running-style grades and keep every style tied for the highest grade. Front wins any tie; pure Pace comes next; Pace with Late or End goes in Pace + Late; End alone or with Late goes in Late + End; pure Late goes in Pure Late. The bucket is automatic for each outfit.

SS is the first-pass baseline. A1 and A2 are authoring priorities for variants; they are independent of the RPG’s class tiers.
