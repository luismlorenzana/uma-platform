The sub-apps

Each sub-app has its own route, its own API routes and its own code folder, and never imports from another one (AP1). A sub-app that isn't built yet has no tile.

Uma Ranker

Every board online at /ranker. Playable Umas ranks all 272 outfits on ten tiers, T1–T10, one art batch each; one place or a whole tier up and down; sisters rank as Umas of their own. The bucket is the source of truth; pnpm ranker:pull keeps the Mac's copy.

RK1 live RK2 after your ranking

Uma Character Art

uma-quest's Album at /art, laid out by the Ranker's ten batches: adult travelers (baseline, keys, faces) and every Uma and sister (portrait, bust, face, glass, chibis, dresses). CA2 adds chibi sprite sets, support card art and each adult set's inputs.

CA1 ready CA2 after CA1

Quest, Farm and Platform Art

Every original asset each one uses, from uma-art and each game's private pack: kits, props, bubbles, icons, foes.

GA1–GA3, later

Publish

Publish from your private authoring. It reads the files from the authoring backup or through a local helper, and moves the Studio's approval pages online.

PB1, later

How it fits

One site and one API. The platform reads what master data and the games publish, and writes only to its own bucket.

The full-body 3D renders (portraits and dresses) and the chibis in master data are already the originals' size, 512 px, so no new store is needed for inputs (AP21).

The cloud, in uma-rpg

The games' pattern (IN1–IN26), with app id platform. An idempotent infra/bootstrap.sh creates it, every command scoped to the project; the active gcloud account never changes.
