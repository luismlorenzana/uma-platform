Why lossy WebP

A scene is only ever shown whole, never drawn over another picture (unlike a portrait's face over its base), so lossless buys nothing visible. Quality 92 is about a fifth of lossless's bytes, and the pack refuses a scene that comes back under 38 dB colour PSNR against its approved PNG.

Checks

  • Pack: each PNG is the approved one (SHA-256 and size), opaque, and decodes back at its size within the floor.
  • Parser: at least one scene, ids unique, names lowercase words joined by hyphens, paths under art/illustrations/.
  • Client: her id, and every scene listed in the release with its bytes and SHA-256.
  • Read-back: release:check decodes every scene: a WebP, no alpha, at its size.

How they get there

1. Approved and pushedChatGPT's scenes, owner-approved; the push adds each to the universe's index as kind: "splash" (P175).pnpm art:push farm-life
2. Moments namedEach scene's moment from the game's request, in the private map.content/authoring/illustrations/farm-life.json
3. PackedFetches the index and the splash PNGs, matches her by name, checks and encodes each.pnpm illustrations:pack
4. CandidateLatest plus the family, every other file carried byte for byte.pnpm release:publish -- --illustrations-only
5. Read backLike a game reads it, against latest.pnpm release:check -- --channel candidate --against latest
6. LatestMoves the channel; the games read it once they pin 1.8.0.pnpm release:publish -- --promote <version>

With --portraits-only too, the dialogue portraits and the illustrations go in one release.
