Enemy keys: the format

How an enemy is drawn, checked and published, for any game: uma-quest's three tiers (its enemy art review, EN1–EN13) written down as one contract. The doc is enemy-keys.md (EK1–EK15), beside the Umas' format.md and pipeline.md; the decision is P152 (uma-quest Q16 part A). Enemies appear by id only, and this page shows no character art: the figure below is the code-drawn test boss.

Tiers and poses

A key pose is one still, not an animation. The game times every motion in code from the fight's cues: a breathe-bob, a lunge, a recoil, a sink.

TierRequiredOptionalCanvasFigureIn a fight
regularbattlebreak512 × 768112 pxA random fight's foe, up to three on the field; her place in the pool sets her role

Figures are heights at uma-quest's 844 × 390 reference stage (EN3). A pose name outside its tier's list is an error.

Canvas and anchors

foot (or hover): where she stands hit: where hits land face: bubbles, status, the camera cast or muzzle: where an attack leaves her

The rules

  • Every pose of an enemy is on her tier's canvas; a boss may widen hers up to 1024 × 1024, all poses with it.
  • A clear border of at least 8 px: no alpha within 8 px of an edge.
  • Facing right, drawn that way, never mirrored.
  • No effects baked in: the game draws glows, flashes and bursts at her anchors.
  • Masters are PNG; the release is lossless WebP.

Anchors, per pose

  • foot or hover, never both: a floating figure hovers.
  • hit and face always.
  • cast or muzzle where she attacks from.
  • Every anchor on the canvas, in canvas pixels from the top left.

A boss's layers

LayerHolds
rearBehind the whole figure: a cape's far side, wings, a rear weapon or rigging
behindBehind her body, in front of rear: a far arm, hair behind the shoulders
bodyHer body, head and costume
frontAcross her body: a near arm, a weapon held across, a cape's near side
overOne more if needed: what overlaps everything (a shield, a sleeve, a ribbon)

3–5 of them, back to front, each the full canvas, each with a pivot it sways about. Only a boss's battle pose has layers; her other poses swap in whole.

Checks

One module, scripts/data/src/enemy-images.ts, used by the pack on the masters and by release:check on the released WebPs. "Re-composite exactly" means to 8-bit rounding: alpha within 1 level, colour weighted by alpha within 1.5 levels of 255. A layer moved by one pixel fails it.

The roster: the enemies set

Ids

  • Numeric, opaque and stable, from 700001 to 799999, clear of outfit, card and character ids.
  • The private ledger content/authoring/tiers/enemy-ids.json keeps them: a unit keeps hers when she moves boards or ranks.
  • A new unit takes next, which only counts up, so an id is never reused; a unit who leaves the boards keeps her ledger entry.
  • 799999 is the code-drawn test enemy's, at the top of the range the ledger never reaches.

An item

{ "id": 700001,
  "franchise": "azurlane",
  "tier": "boss",
  "name": "…" }

Names live only in master data's private release. A game keeps ids in git and reads names at run time.

The release

files/art/enemy-keys/<id>-battle.<hash16>.webp
files/art/enemy-keys/<id>-battle-rear.<hash16>.webp
files/data/art.<hash16>.json                  enemyKeys: { <id>: <manifest> }
const { manifest, art } = await client.load()
const enemies = await client.enemies(manifest)      // or null
const key = await client.enemyKey(manifest, art!, 700123)
const img = await client.file(manifest, key!.poses.battle!.path)

Both are additive in uma-data 1.6.0: enemies is an optional set and enemyKeys an optional index key. The dry run's release parses in every uma-data the games have installed (1.1.0–1.4.0), and each skips both. data:import, assets:import and sprites:pack keep them as they are.

Publishing

The owner's set review approves an enemy; that approval is the go. --enemies-only goes to the candidate channel by default.

1. Packthe roster and every approved key into the local releasepnpm enemies:pack
2. Planlatest plus the set, the keys and a new art indexpnpm release:publish -- --enemies-only --dry-run
3. Candidatelatest doesn't movepnpm release:publish -- --enemies-only
4. Read backlike a game, against latestpnpm release:check -- --channel candidate --against latest
5. Promotethen read latest backpnpm release:publish -- --promote <version>
