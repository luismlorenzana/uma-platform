How it's cut

Half scale, with the first three Umas' numbers. The square is placed from her face, never from a fixed spot, so a base whose head sits a pixel or two apart gets its own square.

Each base of her dialogue-portraits manifest gains a profile; older readers ignore it.

The seasons' outfits are spring, summer, fall and winter.

Review

pnpm profiles:push reads the profiles from the master data channel and sends them, then their index (kind: universe-profiles, with the release's version), to the app's bucket, art/universes/<universe>/profiles/. Uma Character Art shows them on each Uma's page under "Profile pictures". If one looks off, its square goes in content/authoring/profiles/<universe>.json (private), keyed <character id>/<outfit>.
