Art authoring · Uma Cyberpunk · P182

Two initiatives, two approval cycles

Uma Cyberpunk's art is authored in two separate initiatives, each shaped like Farm Life's: a packet in git (method, workflow, templates), a private collection on the owner's machine, an approval record, and an album built by the committed album code. Names, references, images, approvals and every album's words stay local.

Enemies Uma Enemy Art

Enemies and bosses on the Standard, one kit per faction. Packet · Workflow

  1. 1Wired: references, variant brief, prompt draft (enemies/tools/wire.py)
  2. 2A study per round, inside her faction (<faction>/<study>-v00N/)
  3. 3Review page: recognition, the shared look, the faction read, hers, anatomy
  4. 4Owner's verdict: the PNG copied into enemies/approved/ with a manifest entry
  5. 5The enemy album: pnpm art:albums cyberpunk, faction by faction
  6. 6Published on the owner's word: pnpm art:push cyberpunk --enemies --only …, then in Uma Enemy Art, faction by faction

Lieutenants Uma Enemy Art

The three lieutenants who serve each boss: the enemy art's third app (P200), with its own studies, approvals and albums. All 150 cast lieutenants have their folders and Ranker references; the first approved looks are one lieutenant's four, moved from the bosses' side (P201). Packet · Workflow

  1. 1Find her in the lieutenants' cast album: Tier 1 first, Uma by Uma
  2. 2A study inside her own folder (lieutenants/cast/tier-<n>/<NN>-<uma>/<lieutenant>/v00N/)
  3. 3Review: the cast album shows each picture on her card as it is saved
  4. 4Owner's verdict: the PNG copied into lieutenants/approved/ with a manifest entry
  5. 5The albums: pnpm art:albums cyberpunk; her cast card badges it Approved
  6. 6Published on the owner's word (EA4): art:push cyberpunk --enemies, then in Uma Enemy Art under her own name, labelled Lieutenant

The cast folders 250 ready

Every boss and lieutenant the owner cast in the Ranker (Tiers 1–5: 50 Umas, each with one boss and three lieutenants) has a folder in each app that authors her, with her Ranker pictures already in it. pnpm art:cast cyberpunk keeps the folders in step with the Ranker; each app's cast album shows every character, in tier and board order, and every picture added to her folder, fresh on each visit through the Local Art Gallery.

The calls (P182, P185, P186, P200, P201)

1Two separate initiatives, each with its own packet, collection, approval record and album
2The pilots' album is built by the album code from a local album.json; the hand-written page is kept in _retired-gallery-code/
3A character with looks only gets a looks-only album; dialogue portraits and keys are later packets
4The enemy album groups approved looks by faction, in album.json's order; the code names no enemy or faction
5Enemy looks publish to Uma Enemy Art (P183), their own sub-app, never Character Art; on the owner's word
6Characters publish as before, on the owner's word, with art:push cyberpunk
7Casual clothing is its own cycle (P185): its own packet, studies, approval record and album, apart from the combat looks
8Her approved combat look is the likeness anchor; the first approved casual look sets the casual baseline
9Off duty: no armour, helmet, weapon, emitter or Hold mark; her accent leads, her faction in one small piece
10One album code for both enemy collections: enemy-album.mjs takes the collection, its words say the noun and the empty line
11Casual looks publish to Uma Enemy Art, first in her group beside her armour looks (EA3, P186), on the owner's word
12Lieutenants are the third enemy app (P200): their own packet, studies, approvals and albums; blank until authoring starts
13Every cast character has a folder in each app that authors her: bosses in the boss and casual apps, lieutenants in theirs, ordered as the Ranker's cast (tier, Uma)
14Her Ranker pictures are her references, copied byte for byte with their hashes into her folder's references/
15New studies go in her own folder; the first bosses' earlier studies stay where they were
16The cast albums read the folders, so a new picture shows on her card at once through the Local Art Gallery
17art:cast follows the Ranker: re-cast characters move with their work, nothing is deleted, leftovers are reported
18A cast card leads with her pilot suit when one is approved (the album's leadLook); her Ranker reference stays one link away
19A character cast as a lieutenant lives on the lieutenants' side, her approved looks moved there with their history; Uma Enemy Art labels her Lieutenant
