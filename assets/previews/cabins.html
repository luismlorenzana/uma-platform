Cabin furniture, keepsakes and gifts (uma-cyberpunk CY14)

Original pixel art drawn in code in scripts/pixel-art/src/cabins (uma-platform P164, P126/P147), keyed by uma-cyberpunk's item ids (its packages/domain/src/cabins/items.ts at 891e50e). Two uma-art families: cabin-icons (all 48 items, 64 px, eight across, 512 × 384) and cabin-room (the 30 furniture pieces at room size: Bed, Desk and Floor 150 px, Wall and Light 96 px, one row per slot, 900 × 642). Each piece is drawn natively at both sizes from the same geometry, never resampled. Room frames carry mount (floor, wall or ceiling) and foot (where a standing piece meets the floor; a rug's whole cell). No characters, no master data.
