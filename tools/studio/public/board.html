Click a portrait to open it. Drag one onto a row to rank it, or onto another portrait to put it just before. Order within a row is the authoring order. The list follows the search and filters.

What gets saved, and where does it go?

Every pick saves this board to its own file, and the Studio rewrites content/authoring/tiers/plan.json, the complete plan with every board in authoring order. Both are private, on this machine and in the authoring backup; nothing is published or committed.

SSS, SS, S and A are this board’s cast. B, F and unranked leave it, and if a board sits below this one, they appear there. A tier on this board never changes another board.

On an imported boss board, Left out takes a unit off every board (a male character from an unfiltered import, say). Rank it or drag it to a row to bring it back.

References are the original released images, served only from the local release and the private enemy folder.
