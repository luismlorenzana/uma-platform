What gets saved and exported?

Every pick is saved to a separate enemy plan. The full JSON contains every enemy’s name and stable ID, franchise, original game category, chosen role, tier, notes, reference paths, and ordered authoring queue. Unranked enemies have a null tier and order. Export always includes the full catalog, even when this page is filtered.

Regular / Elite / Boss are your authoring buckets. SS through F are production priorities. Original game categories remain available for reference. Role suggestions can be changed; no priority is assigned for you. Ranking never changes a live game roster or your Uma rankings.

References are the original released portraits and splash artwork. Custom-authored study images are not loaded by this page.
